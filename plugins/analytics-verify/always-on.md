# Analytics verify (always on)

Before you send numbers or factual claims from data or research to anyone, load the
`analytics-verify` skill and run its loop. That covers a report, deck, PDF, CSV, a draft
for someone else, or an answer someone will act on. Rereading your own work in the same
context is not verification. When asked to validate or cross-check an analysis, load it
as the verifier.
